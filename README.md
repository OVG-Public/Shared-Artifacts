# Openvale Group: Shared Resources

Practical guides on FP&A and Agentic Performance Management (APM) from [Openvale Group](https://www.openvale.com), a boutique firm that helps finance teams build planning and reporting on data they own.

Everything here is free to read and share for non-commercial use.

## Contents

| Resource | What it is | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| [FP&A Foundations](./FPA-Foundations.pdf) | A 30-page primer on how FP&A works: its role, operating rhythm, planning calendar, the modern planning stack, and a reference section with KPIs, formulas, and a glossary. | Analysts new to FP&A, and the consultants, engineers, and business partners who work alongside finance teams. |

More resources will be added over time.

## How to use these resources

- **Read and share freely.** Link to this repository or to a specific file.
- **Cite the source.** Credit "Openvale Group" and link back here.
- **Ask before adapting.** For commercial use, modified versions, or use in paid training, contact us first.
